GRAPHICS SETTING NOTE
The native texture-quality selector applies 100/50/25 percent decoded RGBA mip-chain reduction before GPU allocation for GX static and palette textures. Changing quality invalidates the GX static/content caches so subsequently resolved textures use the selected allocation. Standalone independent rational-area oracles cover odd dimensions, single-pixel axes, mip retention, invalid inputs, and unchanged source buffers. Live GPU allocation after a menu change, visual quality/performance measurements, and full-game acceptance remain unverified; do not claim those from CPU reducer tests. The lighting and fighter shadow controls are render-only and must not be used to alter gameplay collision or simulation.
